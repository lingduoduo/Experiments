## Metric Change

**I. Clarify**

- timeframe
- baseline compare with what
- active — what kind of active
- ratio: confirm denominator and numerator

**II. Hypothesize**

- Internal: bug, system error, recently launch new features
- external: competitor, user behavior change

**III. Diagnose**

1. **Time: How has the metric varied over time?**

   1. how has performance been trending over the last few weeks? it may be just a normal
   2. seasonal effects
   3. sudden or gradual
   4. any more pronounced week or time of day

   How? look at historical data for the metrics to see how it’s changed over time

2. **Region: concentrate on certain regions or happen across the board**

   1. segmentation

3. **Other Internal Products**

   1. is this change specific to one product or company-wide
   2. other metrics changed

4. **Platform**

   1. Mobile v.s. Desktop
   2. Mac v.s. Window
   3. Android vs IOS

5. **Industry & Competitors**

   1. if a new competitor entered the space around the same time (or an existing competitor released a new offering)?

6. **Cannibalization**

   1. *If the metric for a product is decreasing, is it because another product we offer is cannibalizing engagement? Alternatively, if the metric in question is increasing, are we cannibalizing from our other offerings?*
   2. • Analyze key metrics across other products and features to determine if a fall in our metric of interest coincides with a rise in the metric of another product.
   3. • **Is the increase in usage of the new feature equal to the decline of the original feature?**

7. **Segmentation**

- Age, sex, education
- Power users versus casual users
- New users, Returning user, existing users
- New users versus existing users

  | Metric                         | Description                                                  |
  | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
  | Impressions                    | The number of times your Pins or ads were on screen.         |
  | Engagements                    | The total number of engagements on your Pins, including saves, Pin clicks, outbound clicks, carousel card swipes, secondary creative (collections) clicks, and Idea Pin forward/backward swipes. |
  | Pin clicks                     | The total number of clicks on your Pin or ad to content on or off of Pinterest. |
  | Outbound clicks                | The number of times people perform actions that lead them to a destination off Pinterest. |
  | Outbound click rate            | The total number of clicks to the destination URL associated with your Pin divided by the total number of times your Pins were on screen. |
  | Video views                    | The number of views where at least 2 seconds were watched with 50% of the video in view. |
  | Average play time              | The average time someone spent playing your video.           |
  | Saves                          | The number of times people saved your video Pin to a board.  |
  | Pin click rate                 | The total number of clicks on your Pin or ad to content on or off Pinterest divided by the total number of times your Pins or ads were on screen. |
  | Played at 95%                  | The number of times your video was viewed to 95% of its length. |
  | Total play time                | The total play time for your video, measured in minutes.     |
  | 10-second views                | The number of times your video was viewed for at least 10 seconds or for the full length if shorter than 10 seconds. |
  | Engagement rate                | The total engagements with your Pins divided by the total number of times your Pins were seen. Engagements include saves, Pin clicks, and outbound clicks. |
  | Save rate                      | The total saves of your Pins divided by the total number of times your Pins were on screen. |
  | Total audience                 | The total number of people who have seen or engaged with your Pins. |
  | Total engaged audience         | The number of people who have engaged with your Pins.        |
  | Monthly total audience         | The total number of people who have seen or engaged with your Pins in a rolling 30-day window up to and including each day. |
  | Monthly total engaged audience | The total number of people who have engaged with your Pins in a rolling 30-day window up to and including each day. |
  | Monthly views                  | The number of times your published Pins and Pins saved from your claimed domain or accounts were on screen in the last 30 days. |
